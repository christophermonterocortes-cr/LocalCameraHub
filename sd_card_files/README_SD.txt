TEMU / ANYKA AK3918 CAMERA - LOCAL RTSP UNLOCK GUIDE
======================================================
These files have been generated to enable local RTSP streaming on your Temu camera (192.168.0.4)
without relying on any proprietary cloud apps or third-party servers.

DEVICE DETAILS:
- Model: Anyka AKWIPC / Xiao K Internet Camera
- Chipset: Anyka AK3918E
- MAC Address: a8:4f:a4:70:3e:7d (China Dragon Technology)
- Assigned IP: 192.168.0.4

HOW TO USE THESE FILES:
1. Insert a standard MicroSD card (FAT32 formatted, 32GB or smaller recommended) into your PC.
2. Copy all files from this folder directly onto the root directory of the SD card:
   - anyka_cfg.ini
   - run.sh
   - test.sh
3. Safely eject the MicroSD card from your PC.
4. Power off your Temu camera by unplugging its USB power cable.
5. Insert the MicroSD card into the camera's slot.
6. Plug the camera back in and allow 30-45 seconds for it to boot.
7. The camera will execute the script, unlock port 554, and start streaming live video!
8. Open Local Camera Hub at http://localhost:5000 to view your live stream.
